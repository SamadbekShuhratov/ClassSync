Everything you do at the front of the room, on every desk

One console for the whole lesson. Students see what you send instantly, whether they joined from the desktop helper or a browser tab.

Open a website on every computer

Paste a link and press send. It opens on each student's machine in under a second — no spelling, no typos, no "which site was it?".

Share your screen live

Show a window, a tab or your whole display. Students watch full-screen, with a quality setting for slow Wi-Fi.

Collect assignments

Open an assignment and an upload page appears for everyone. See who has handed in, then download it all as one ZIP.

Focus mode

One switch covers student screens with a message so the room looks up.

Announcements

Pin a short instruction to the top of every student's screen.

Stays inside your school

Runs on a teacher laptop. Files and names never leave your local network.
